How to Cherish Your Crochet

Keep your fibers soft and vibrant for decades with these simple slow-fashion tips.

1. Gentle Hand Wash

Submerge in cool water with mild wool detergent. Avoid harsh wringing or machine agitators.

2. Lay Flat to Dry

Roll in a clean towel to absorb excess moisture, then reshape and dry flat away from direct sun.

3. Fold, Never Hang

Hangers stretch heavy woven stitches. Fold gently and store with a cedar block or lavender sprig.
